# Chapter 03 — The Birth

Spirit City woke early. Bells from Douluo Hall, smoke from kitchens, and the low murmur of soul masters lining up before Pope Hall gate. Word had gone out three days before, on copied paper, black ink still smelling.

Martial Soul True Classification. First volume. Free to read.

The line at the gate went around the corner by mid-morning. Farmers, city boys, branch hall masters, two Holy Hall bishops who had ridden all night. Ghost stood at gate, black, silent, handing out copies. Chrysanthemum stood behind him, gold, sharp-eyed, watching faces.

Inside copies, plain brush, steady hand.

Tool. Beast. Body. Element. Celestial Body.

Low. Mid. High. Top. Supreme, Ultimate, God.

Hao Tian Hammer just Top.

That line stopped men mid-page. A blacksmith in line read it twice, out loud, and looked up at Pope Hall like hall had spoken heresy. A Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon elder two streets over read it and threw copy down, then picked it up again.

One lakh red. Two lakh orange gold. Great soul beast.

Body plus spiritual power importance. Realms with points. How to choose rings.

Every line true. That was why it shocked. Not because it was loud, but because it was true and had never been written plainly before.

By noon, Spirit City was loud. By evening, pigeons left with copies tied to legs, heading to Heaven Dou, to Star Luo, to Main Halls major cities, Sub-Halls medium cities, branch halls every city landmark.

Pope Hall inside, he sat at desk. Chest warm. Dantian solid. Six wings faint behind, white-gold, brighter than before. Rings — Two Yellow Two Purple Five Black — pulsed with breath, part of body. Head and Torso bones one lakh each, fully absorbed, part of body. Body tough as angel. Spiritual sea tough as angel.

He was ninety-six. Only one level after seclusion, but quality largely stronger. No human in this era had soul core. He had True Angel Core. Difference huge.

He listened to murmur outside. Truth and reputation. Spirit Hall gains truth and reputation as true holy land of knowledge, not just power. People respect, worship. Faith power collected slowly. Secret reason guild codex — faith from belief worship truth reputation slowly creates God position of own — adaptation talent will make God position part when true divine later — True Divine can make God position part everything God level. He kept that secret. Only wrote Angel God needs pure faith.

Ink dry on second volume. How to choose rings. First limit four twenty-three second optimum seven sixty-four. Body importance pool deepened pathways widened vitality beast bodies robust even without release. Spiritual power importance Spirit Origin zero to ninety-nine Connection hundred to four ninety-nine Sea five hundred to four thousand nine ninety-nine Abyss five thousand to nineteen thousand Domain twenty thousand to forty-nine thousand Divine Origin fifty thousand plus God King points importance for soul core Sea first Abyss Titled Domain Limit how to increase Purple Demon Eyes like training. Both need high to form soul core to become Limit to become God.

He did not write soul core in first books. All but not soul core thing. Too advanced. No human in this era had soul core. Shocking if shared. Would share later after guild established.

Ghost came in, black, silent. Bowed.

"His Holiness, copies gone. More copying."

He nodded. "Copy more. Send to Holy Hall capitals, Main Halls, Sub-Halls. Let them read. Then public. Free."

Ghost bowed, black disappearing.

Next, Elder Hall.

Elder Hall was round, stone seats in circle, high windows, holy light faint. Twenty seats, most filled. Seven front higher — Worshipers. Qian Daoliu not there — Great Worship rarely came, stayed in Worship Hall with Angel statue. Golden Crocodile sat front, old, scarred, ninety-eight, only step away from ninety-nine, second strongest after Qian Daoliu. He did not have soul core — no one in this era had soul core — no human had soul core — OC was first human in this era with True Angel Core.

Debate about codex. Some elders said Hao Tian Hammer just Top would offend Clear Sky Clan. Some said Body type rare Celestial Body rare would cause panic. Some corrupt elders said free knowledge weakens Spirit Hall taxes.

Golden Crocodile raised hand. Hall quiet.

"Truth," he said. Voice old, hoarse. "Spirit Hall stood as sacred land because Pope Hall and Douluo Hall. If we speak truth and truth is true, people worship. If people worship, Angel God gains faith. Faith is power. Corruption weakens faith. I support codex. I support strict rules."

Other Worshipers — Blue Lizard, Light Feather — glanced, then nodded. They were ninety-six, led by Qian Daoliu. Qian Daoliu had not come, but his will was there — son finally awakened, finally understood Angel God path, not Pope politics but Angel. Fatherly hope, old father saw son fail fifty years impurities bloodline not pure rings not fully his now sees improvement six wings brighter white-gold holy light stronger faint golden radiance purification — supports guild codex because benefits Angel God — supports strict rules because corruption weakens Spirit Hall Angel God needs pure faith — natural support not blind but because benefits Angel God and family. He had NEVER seen soul core before — first time seeing — shocked but hopeful — wants to learn how to form first core — cannot re-condense second core because he has no first core yet.

Debate lasted hours. At end, they passed second foundation — Soul Master Guild open to all not just Spirit Hall branches across continent capitals major cities medium every city like Spirit Hall branch halls but open knowledge — purpose share true knowledge gain truth reputation Spirit Hall true holy land knowledge not just power people respect worship faith power collected secretly collect faith power slowly create God position own.

Bibi Dong sat on bed, blanket around shoulders, knees to chest. Hatred in eyes, but less sharp now. More tired. Belly small, then not small. She had not eaten much first month, then more. Pills from small bottle helped — body, future child — he knew via meta knowledge she was pregnant with Qian Renxue, would give birth hidden, Qian Renxue born six-winged angel, future Angel God, tragic end godhood shattered stuck at eighty-nine if not loved — he wanted be real father provide all resources.

She did not answer first months. Looked at wall.

Belly big now. She walked in courtyard, guards at distance for safety, as he had ordered. She did not thank. Did not need to. Victim does not thank attacker because attacker healed physical pain and provided resources. That would not be natural. She walked, hand on belly sometimes, hatred still, but also tired.
