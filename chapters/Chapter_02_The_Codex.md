# Chapter 02 — The Codex

Pope Hall smelled of old paper and holy light. The desk was stone, heavy, carved with six wings. Behind it, angel statues stood with swords down. Light fell through high windows in shafts, dust moving slow inside them.

He sat at the desk. Chest warm. Dantian solid. Six wings faint behind, brighter than before. White-gold feathers, light at edges. Soul rings — Two Yellow, Two Purple, Five Black — pulsed with breath, not just external light now, but part of body. Head and Torso bones, one lakh each, fully absorbed, part of body. Body tough as angel. Spiritual sea tough as angel.

He was ninety-six. Only one level after seclusion, but quality largely stronger. No human in this era had soul core. He had True Angel Core. Difference huge. Combat power and recovery significantly stronger.

He picked up brush. Ink black. Paper white. He started writing.

First line: Martial Soul True Classification.

He wrote slow. Brush steady. Hand did not shake. Fifty years Pope memories helped — knew how to hold brush, how to write decrees. Earth memories helped — knew what continent did not know.

He wrote types.

Tool. Beast. Body. Element. Celestial Body.

Tool — Clear Sky Hammer, Seven Kill Sword, tools as martial soul. Beast — Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon, wolf, tiger, beasts as martial soul. Body — rare, body parts as martial soul, own body as martial soul, very rare, strong, Body Sect in future. Element — fire, water, wind, earth, light, darkness, elements as martial soul, control element. Celestial Body — sun, moon, star, rare, celestial bodies as martial soul, sun moon martial soul very rare strong.

He wrote levels.

Low. Mid. High. Top. Supreme / Ultimate / God.

Low — ordinary, common tools, weak beasts, innate zero to three, sickle, hoe, weak rabbit. Mid — better, decent tools beasts, innate four to six, better sword, wolf. High — good, strong tools beasts, innate seven to eight, strong beast good tool. Top — top-tier, Hao Tian Hammer, Blue Lightning Tyrant Dragon, innate nine to ten, strong. Supreme / Ultimate / God — god-tier, Six-Winged Angel, Sea God Trident, innate ten plus full, Angel God bloodline can become God.

He paused at Hao Tian Hammer.

Everyone thought Hao Tian Hammer strongest. Canon thought. But true rank was Top, not God. Strong because techniques — Hao Tian Nine Jue, Disorder Splitting Wind Hammer, many techniques, not just rank. That would shock continent. That was true. He wrote it. Brush did not hesitate.

He wrote soul rings.

White ten years. Yellow hundred. Purple thousand. Black ten thousand. Red one lakh — one lakh years gives red ring. Orange, orange gold — two lakhs years old soul beast calls great soul beast, orange or orange gold, two lakh orange, three lakh orange gold, later eras. One lakh gives red plus soul bone chance. When soul beast over one lakh hunted, guaranteed drop one lakh bone for body part killer does not possess unless already has all six.

He wrote soul bones.

Six positions — head, torso, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg — plus external rare outside six can evolve. Once absorbed cannot be separated unless owner killed automatically drops ownerless. Limb bones exception when Titled Douluo can sever limb forcibly extract at cost soul power decreasing ten levels. Torso strongest, then skull, then right arm, left arm, left leg, right leg. Fully absorbed becomes part of body no longer external improves physique. External rare can be attached outside, Eight Spider Lances can evolve with holder.

He wrote body and spiritual power importance.

Body — how important physique, need high physique to absorb higher year ring to form soul core to handle Limit Douluo. Pool deepened, pathways widened, vitality. Beast bodies robust even without release. Importance of body.

Spiritual power — how important spiritual power creates realm with points. Spirit Origin zero to ninety-nine. Connection hundred to four ninety-nine. Sea five hundred to four thousand nine ninety-nine. Abyss five thousand to nineteen thousand. Domain twenty thousand to forty-nine thousand. Divine Origin fifty thousand plus. God King. Points importance for soul core. Sea for first core, Abyss for Titled, Domain for Limit. How to increase — Purple Demon Eyes like training.

Both need high to form soul core to become Limit to become God. He was very high spiritual power because ninety-five to ninety-six plus True Angel Core plus adaptation talent purification plus one lakh head bone Angel's Wisdom — golden ripples, spirit eye pupil — much higher than normal ninety-six.

He did not write soul core in first book. Per instruction — all but not soul core thing — first codex excludes soul core too advanced for this era — no human has soul core in this era, shocking if shared, so first codex shares martial soul types, levels, ring ages, spiritual realms, body importance, how to choose rings, external soul bone, etc. Soul core shared later after guild established.

He wrote how to choose rings.

How to choose compatible with martial soul — Angel choose holy light fire not poison. How to absorb need high physique spiritual power first limit four hundred twenty-three years second optimum seven hundred sixty-four years etc. Many things cultivation methods foundation quality over quantity.

He wrote until hand tired. Ink finished, then more ink. Paper piled. Stack grew.

Outside, Spirit City, sacred land for all soul masters because Pope Hall and Douluo Hall. Worship Hall above Pope Hall only Douluo qualified. Elder Hall between Pope Hall and Douluo Hall core of power decides major decisions even Pope only three votes all elders can dismiss Pope if all pass all elders Title Douluo around twenty seven led by Qian Daoliu at ninety-sixth level Worshipers — but actually Qian Daoliu ninety-nine Limit, Golden Crocodile ninety-eight second, others.

Ghost came in, black, silent. He bowed.

"His Holiness, elders waiting."

He nodded. Stood. Papers stacked. Chest warm, dantian solid, rotating absorbing external power.

Elder Hall was round, stone seats in circle, high windows, holy light faint. Twenty seats, most filled. Seven front seats higher — Worshipers. Qian Daoliu not there — Great Worship rarely came to Elder Hall, stayed in Worship Hall with Angel statue. Golden Crocodile sat front, old, scarred, ninety-eight, only step away from ninety-nine, second strongest after Qian Daoliu. He did not have soul core — no one in this era had soul core — no human had soul core — OC was first human in this era with True Angel Core.

Chrysanthemum and Ghost stood behind Pope seat.

He walked in. Six wings faint behind, white-gold. Holy aura dense, powerful, like Seraphim. Golden Crocodile lifted eyes, felt it, frowned. That aura — chest warm core, dantian solid rotating — that was soul core. Never seen before in this era. No one had. First time seeing. He was surprised. Wanted to learn.

Other elders felt too. Whispers.

He sat.

"I called you for sometime," he said. Voice calm, steady. "I plan to create strict rules and end corruption in Spirit Hall, first foundation."

Silence.

An elder with gray beard — Light Eagle Douluo, ninety-two — leaned forward. "His Holiness, corruption —"

Another elder — Stone Arm Douluo, ninety-one — cut in. "Spirit Hall has always—"

Chrysanthemum glanced at Ghost. Corruption touched many elders. Some elders took offerings from branch halls. Some sold soul beast information. Pope only had three votes. All elders could dismiss Pope if all passed. That was canon — Elder Hall core power decides major decisions.

Golden Crocodile raised hand. Hall quiet.

"Let His Holiness speak," he said. Voice old, hoarse. He looked at Pope, holy aura dense powerful like Seraphim, growth staggering, True Angel Core chest warm dantian solid, phenomena golden vortex white-gold ripples six wings faint angel pupil holy pillar Angel Domain expansion outer elders notice — first time seeing soul core, never seen before, no human in this era had soul core — shocked but curious. "What strict rules?"

He spoke.

"Branch halls take extra taxes. Main Halls sell hunting spots. Some elders take cut. That weakens Spirit Hall. Spirit Hall is sacred land for soul masters because Pope Hall and Douluo Hall. If corrupt, people do not worship. If people do not worship, Angel God loses faith."

He did not say his secret reason — faith power — slowly collect faith power to create God position of own — secret reason guild codex — adaptation talent will make God position part when true divine later — True Divine can make God position part everything God level. He kept that secret. Only said Angel God needs pure faith.

Elders debated. Some corrupt opposed because hurt them. Some clean supported because strengthened Spirit Hall. That was natural political body, not immediate yes.

Golden Crocodile listened, then nodded slow.

"Faith," he said. "Angel God needs pure faith. Corruption weakens faith. I support."

Other Worshipers — Blue Lizard, Light Feather — glanced, then nodded. They were ninety-six, led by Qian Daoliu. Qian Daoliu had not come, but his will was there — son finally awakened, finally understood Angel God path, not Pope politics but Angel. Fatherly hope, old father saw son fail fifty years impurities bloodline not pure rings not fully his now sees improvement six wings brighter white-gold holy light stronger faint golden radiance purification — supports guild codex because benefits Angel God — supports strict rules because corruption weakens Spirit Hall Angel God needs pure faith — natural support not blind but because benefits Angel God and family. He had NEVER seen soul core before — first time seeing — shocked but hopeful — wants to learn how to form first core — cannot re-condense second core because he has no first core yet.

Debate lasted hours. At end, they passed first foundation — strict rules end corruption, first step, not all yet, sometime needed. Pope only three votes, but with Golden Crocodile and Worshipers support, plus purer bloodline True Angel Core much stronger than normal ninety-six holy aura dense powerful like Seraphim, gave weight.

He returned to Pope Hall, wrote more.

Second book — Soul Master Guild — open to all not just Spirit Hall — branches across continent capitals major cities medium every city like Spirit Hall branch halls but open knowledge — purpose share true knowledge gain truth and reputation for Spirit Hall — Spirit Hall becomes true holy land of knowledge not just power — people respect worship faith power collected — secretly collect faith power slowly to create God position own.

He wrote guild structure — headquarters Spirit City — Pope Hall + Worship Hall + Elder Hall support — branches two empires capitals Heaven Dou Star Luo major cities medium every city — members any soul master can join get codex guidance — elders Spirit Hall elders manage initially later more.

He finished first volume — Martial Soul True Classification — stack of paper, ink dry, holy light faint on pages.

He called Ghost.

"Copy this. Send to Worship Hall, Elder Hall, Holy Hall capitals, Main Halls major cities. Let them read. Then public."

Ghost bowed, took stack, black disappearing.

Next, Bibi Dong.

Saintess room, locked, guards per old order — imprisoned to prevent harming fetus monitored day and night until birth — canon order still holds for fetus safety but OC gave order keep safe no punishment provide resources give space — guards outside for safety not punishment.

He knocked. No answer. Opened.

Bibi Dong sat on bed, blanket around shoulders, knees to chest. Hatred in eyes, but less pain now — physical healing done yesterday — body healed, physical pain gone, emotional wound remained. She had not eaten much.

He stood at door, three steps away. Quiet low talk. Not cold but did not know how to talk in relationship — Earth single virgin never before although had Qian Xun Ji fifty years experience and others things but still you can understand — so quite and low talk because did not know how to.

He was genuinely good person, although did not accept or said that he thinks himself he do what's he wanted but actually corely good guy — not even hurt without reason let alone kill without reason — corely good guy — never hurts without reason never kills without reason — tolerance extreme never kills Bibi Dong in any condition — genuinely good person core.

He said, quiet low voice, from door not close, "You need to eat."

She did not answer. Looked at wall.

He placed tray on table near door, not close. Food, warm soup, bread, plus small bottle — pills — for body, for future child — he knew via meta knowledge she was pregnant with Qian Renxue, would give birth hidden, Qian Renxue born six-winged angel, future Angel God, tragic end godhood shattered stuck at eighty-nine if not loved — he wanted be real father provide all resources.

"Resources provided," he said, quiet low talk. "Guards outside for safety not punishment. No one enters without your permission except food medicine. If you need anything, tell guards. If you want walk in courtyard, allow, guards at distance for safety."

She did not answer. Held blanket tighter.

He left small pouch — soul bone safe house via Qian family secret — pills for pregnancy — safe house via Qian family — respects hate — does not force wife role — does not force love — Earth single virgin Gaurav Meena / Càn Róng so matters body memories own — considers wife genuinely — guilt attraction tolerance extreme.

He closed door.

Guards bowed. "Yes, His Holiness."

He told guards, "Provide resources secretly. Pills safe house via Qian family. Keep safe. No punishment. Give space."

Guards glanced each other, bowed.

He returned to Pope Hall — desk angel statues holy light core warm chest chest warm like dragon core dantian solid rotating absorbing external soul power.

He sat. Started second volume — How to choose soul rings and many things — first limit four twenty-three second optimum seven sixty-four etc — body plus spiritual power importance — spiritual realms with points Spirit Origin zero to ninety-nine Connection hundred to four ninety-nine Sea five hundred to four thousand nine ninety-nine Abyss five thousand to nineteen thousand Domain twenty thousand to forty-nine thousand Divine Origin fifty thousand plus — how to increase Purple Demon Eyes like training — pool deepened pathways widened — beast bodies robust even without release — many things.

Outside, Spirit City, holy light, Spirit City sacred land for soul masters. Elder Hall debate still going among themselves — some corrupt elders opposed because hurt them — some clean supported — natural political body — goes naturally per butterfly effects — not forced.

Inside Bibi Dong room, she looked at tray, then at bottle, hatred remained but confusion flickered — old Qian Xun Ji would not provide pills safe house would punish lock harder — this one healed provided resources gave space — she did not eat yet — held blanket — physical pain gone but emotional wound remained — that was natural — victim does not forgive because healed — hatred remains — she held blanket knuckles white.

He wrote, holy light, breath steady, blood warm, bone steady — embodied only — sweat, blood heat, bone heat, chest warm, dantian solid, six wings brighter, golden radiance faint.

First book would shock continent — Hao Tian Hammer just Top level strong because techniques — Six-Winged Angel God tier — Body type rare Celestial Body rare — one lakh red two lakh great soul beast orange gold — external soul bone — spiritual realms with points — body plus spiritual importance — how to choose rings — many things — all true — shocking because true — Spirit Hall would gain truth and reputation true holy land knowledge not just power people respect worship faith power collected slowly — secretly one reason was to collect faith power slowly to create God position of own — faith from belief worship truth reputation slowly creates God position — adaptation talent will make God position part when true divine later — True Divine can make God position part everything God level — secret.
