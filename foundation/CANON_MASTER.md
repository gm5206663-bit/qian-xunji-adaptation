## 2. What a soul master is

- **Martial Souls** (武魂, also "Spirits"/"Battle Spirits") are innate
  supernatural manifestations; **every person normally has one**, inherited
  down family lines (the stronger parent's soul tends to win); only those who
  awaken **soul power** can cultivate as soul masters [canon-wiki].
- **Awakening** happens at **about six years of age**, through a formal
  ceremony that manifests the soul and measures innate soul power. Most people
  awaken **without** soul power and cannot cultivate [canon-wiki]. The
  ordinary maximum innate soul power is rank 10 — **Innate Full Soul Power**
  [canon-wiki]. (Legendary bloodlines break it: the Six-Winged Angel is born
  with **innate full soul power at the 20th level** — see §7.)
- **The ladder** (1–99, then godhood at 100) [canon-novel; corroborated
  canon-wiki: "the apex of this profession is the Titled Douluo, attained at
  level 90 with the ninth soul ring"; Titled Douluo = 90–99, each holding a
  unique title recorded forever]:
  | Levels | Rank |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1–10 | Soul Scholar |
  | 11–20 | Soul Master |
  | 21–30 | Soul Grandmaster |
  | 31–40 | Soul Elder |
  | 41–50 | Soul Ancestor |
  | 51–60 | Soul King |
  | 61–70 | Soul Emperor |
  | 71–80 | Soul Sage |
  | 81–90 | Soul Douluo |
  | 91–99 | **Titled Douluo** |
  | 100 | god |
- **Peerless Douluo** (99): the three great peak masters of the era — **Qian
  Daoliu, Tang Chen, Bo Saixi** [canon-baike].
- **Cultivation is measured primarily by soul power rank**, but real strength
  also turns on soul rings, soul skills, physique, spiritual power, blood
  essence, comprehension, and equipment [canon-wiki].
- **Classifying systems** (specializations): Power Attack, Defense, Control,
  Agility Attack, Food, Auxiliary — plus Evil/Dark soul masters who use
  forbidden methods [canon-wiki].
- **Soul soul qualities** run Trash → High-Level → Super → Ultimate; special
  classes: God-Bestowed, Divine Beast, Mutant (with Evil and **Twin** as
  mutant forms) [canon-wiki].
- **Twin Martial Souls**: an exceptionally rare mutation; occurs when parents
  have different souls of sufficiently similar quality; **Bibi Dong is the
  second twin-soul person in nearly a century** [canon-wiki; canon-baike].
  Twin-soul masters face multi-ring tolerance overload — Bibi Dong is the
  first to overcome it [canon-baike].
- **Development paths**: mutation (form changes), evolution (soul grows),
  secondary awakening (a dormant second soul or bloodline step), and
  **Martial Soul Recognition** [canon-wiki]. At **rank 70** a master can use
  the **Martial Soul True Body (Avatar)** — the soul's full manifestation
  [canon-wiki].
- **Adjacent cultivation systems** named by canon: Spiritual Power, Blood
  Essence, Soul Bones, and (a **later era** development) Soul Cores
  [canon-wiki; soul cores are [later] — ours is the era's first and a story
  anomaly per R7, see `SKILLS_CANON.md`].

## 2b. Soul cores — the full mechanics, tagged by era [canon-wiki]

> Inserted 2026-10-07 (the author's second-core order, R27); sections 3–13
> keep their numbers. Scope corrected the same day — the record below is
> franchise-wide and era-tagged.

- **Era origin — the load-bearing fact:** soul cores are **not SL1-era
  canon**. The method is **created in the SL2 era**: Huo Yuhao watched Di
  Tian fight, studied how the beast-king's soul power circulated ("like
  linked black holes"), built the first human core from it, and taught it at
  Shrek so it could pass down [canon-wiki + franchise summaries: "in Soul
  Land I no one created a soul core; it belongs to Soul Land II"]. **Our
  world (2619) therefore has none, and no one to teach it** — the chapter
  line "No one in this age carried even one center" now rests on the
  franchise record; the OC's method is **his books' knowledge** [design
  riding these receipts].
- **Huo Yuhao's three cores — the canon template [SL2]:** 1st **Spiritual
  core** — upper dantian (between the brows), formed at his Soul Sage
  breakthrough, stabilized by the Skydream Iceworm; 2nd **Ultimate Ice
  core** — middle dantian (chest), at his Titled breakthrough; 3rd
  **Spatial core** — lower dantian, on the path to Limit Douluo; first
  human yin-yang (and only triple-core) holder [canon-wiki: Soul_Cores ref
  list ch458.2/459.1/459.3, 521, 587.3, 619.1/.2 + franchise summary].

- **What a core is:** highly compressed soul power solidified into a stable,
  continuously rotating center inside the cultivator. Once formed it pulls
  origin energy in on its own, hugely raising soul-power recovery, cultivation
  efficiency, and the stamina to keep techniques running [canon-wiki].
- **Requirements and danger:** spiritual power (to control the process),
  physical resilience, soul-power density, and control. If the forming core
  loses stability, the compressed energy is extremely dangerous — Elder Xuan
  warned a body could be destroyed [canon-wiki].
- **Not a fixed rank:** era and person change the normal stage. In the SL2
  era a core was thought an ability of extremely high masters — Elder Xuan
  called a Soul Sage's attempt almost impossible — yet Huo Yuhao succeeded at
  seven rings, stabilized by the Skydream Iceworm's origin power (a pure
  fusion of soul power and spiritual power). By the SL3 era, a first core was
  the normal mark of the seven-ringed Soul Sage stage; Tang Wulin began
  condensing one **before rank 60** [canon-wiki].
- **Three human locations (and the order constraint):** between the
  eyebrows (upper dantian), the chest (middle dantian), the abdominal
  dantian (lower). Each completed core constrains the space and circulation
  left for the next; canon's template fills them upper → middle → lower
  [canon-wiki: Soul_Cores; Huo Yuhao's layout, [SL2]].
- **A vortex comes first:** before a core solidifies, the cultivator develops
  a soul-power vortex that rotates and draws in heaven-and-earth origin
  energy — the transitional stage that lets exceptional people form cores
  early [canon-wiki].
- **Types:** standard soul-power cores; a **Spiritual Soul Core** (tied to
  spiritual power and the spiritual sea); elemental cores (Ultimate Ice,
  spatial); and **Blood Essence cores** — the Dragon Core family — which run
  as parallel structures from bloodline power and are not automatically under
  the ordinary cores' limits (Tang Wulin: Dragon Core in the chest; his
  ordinary soul core later; the two stayed separate and supported each other
  through his life force) [canon-wiki].
- **The second core — two canon methods:** **Resonance** (the safer path:
  study the first core's rotation and frequency, bring the second into
  harmony) and **Yin Yang Complement** (the harder path: the second core
  rotates against the first, causing severe interference and pressure during
  formation; if completed, the two energies endlessly collide, purify,
  compress, and circulate — far more powerful than resonance). Di Tian had
  achieved yin-yang dual cores; **Huo Yuhao became the first known human** to
  do it. He later formed a **third**, spatial-type core in the dantian on the
  path toward the highest mortal realm [canon-wiki].
- **What a second core buys (canon terms):** its hallmark is Hyper Douluo
  cultivation and it is called an essential step toward Limit Douluo; Tang
  Wulin, having just entered Titled Douluo, was held to face **no cultivation
  bottleneck before at least rank 95** [canon-wiki; [SL3]].
- **Where Ch04 sits (consistency check):** the first core formed at the
  95→96 passage (Ch01) and the second at 96 — inside canon's own window:
  SL3's Tang Wulin formed his second at his Titled breakthrough, and canon
  calls two cores the **Hyper Douluo** hallmark (95+); the spiritual-sea
  second carried "no bottleneck before at least rank 95" [canon-wiki
  ch1395/1396]. The OC sits below that wall, in a world where no human has
  any core at all.
- **Bridge Between Heaven and Earth (天地之桥):** a connection through which
  separate centers communicate and circulate into one greater flow. Tang
  Wulin's yin-yang vortex between his two cores grew into the Bridge; in
  Soul Land V, Tang San's soul core (dantian) and Spiritual Core (spiritual
  sea) linked through the spine into the Bridge — the dual-core resonance
  that let his bloodline transformations evolve [canon-wiki; [later] eras].
- **Beasts:** powerful soul beasts can carry cores too, but a second is extra
  hard for them — the yin-yang method is their only path (Di Tian; the Evil
  Eye Tyrant Emperor) [canon-wiki].
- **Trivia note:** outside readers compare cores to the jindan/golden core of
  other cultivation fiction; that comparison is not canon terminology
  [canon-wiki]. [Later]



- A **soul ring** is the energy body a soul beast leaves on death, sacrifice,
  or contract. **Only the one who delivers the killing blow can absorb it**,
  and only at a **bottleneck (rank 10, 20, 30 …)** [canon-wiki].
- Soul ring level reflects the beast's **cultivation, not literal age** —
  beasts mature, not just age; physical features mark rank [canon-wiki].
- **The five ordinary levels** [canon-wiki]:
  - **White** — 10+ years
  - **Yellow** — 100+ years
  - **Purple** — 1,000+ years
  - **Black** — 10,000+ years
  - **Red** — 100,000+ years
- Beyond: **Orange** — 200,000+ years (Fierce/Great Beast rings) and **Gold**
  — 1,000,000+ years [canon-wiki; both are effectively [later]-era phenomena,
  but the color table is canon-wide].
- **God-bestowed soul rings** — no attributes, granted on completing a god's
  trials (Sea God Island's test-takers; Bo Saixi) [canon-wiki]. God-level
  rings are exclusive to gods [canon-wiki].
- **Ownerless rings** (stored from beasts without a killer) require special
  soul tools — a later-era Tang Sect method [canon-wiki, [later]].
- Also canon: soul **shock** on absorbing an over-age ring, **optimal ring
  combinations by rank**, **age ascension** (feeding rings to age them — an
  SL2+ practice), and ring **camouflage** [canon-wiki]. House rulings s33
  (Nth ring at Nth decade; thousand-year second = pedigree anomaly;
  thousand-year first = death) sit on top of these tables — see
  `SKILLS_CANON.md`.
- **A beast's rings drop on death.** Our house law (SB-11: every creature is
  a soul beast) has no canon contradiction; canon's "normal animals" are not a
  category on the Douluo Continent [canon-wiki].

## 4. Soul bones and the beast economy

- A soul beast can leave a **soul bone** — canon classifies them by body part
  (skull/head, torso, arms, legs; six slots across a body), quality, and age;
  a bone's age matches the beast's cultivation [canon-novel; receipted in
  `SKILLS_CANON.md`]. **External soul bones** (beyond the six) are canon
  [canon-novel]. A 100,000-year beast's bone is the prize of the age (the
  project's head + torso 100k bones are story canon per R7/R13).
- Beast cultivation brackets mirror the ring table (ten / hundred / thousand
  / ten-thousand / hundred-thousand year beasts). Every hundred thousand
  years a beast faces a **heavenly tribulation**; failure kills it, and some
  survivors take **human form** [canon-wiki, mostly [later] phenomena].
- The **Great Star Dou Forest** holds the era's 100,000-year titans — canon
  names the **Sky Blue Bull Python** and the **Titan Giant Ape** (Bibi Dong
  hunted them in 2653, §1) [canon-baike].
- **A Yin, the Blue Silver Emperor**: a 100,000-year beast-lady; the 2631
  hunt, her sacrifice, and Tang Hao's stand are §9 [canon-wiki].

## 5. Spirit Hall — the complete institution

Terminology note: the wiki translates 武魂殿 as "Martial Soul Hall"; the story
uses "Spirit Hall" / "the Hall". Both are the same body. Spirit Hall is the
continental superpower of the era — see §11 for its standing among empires.

- **The head:** the **Supreme Pontiff (Pope)**. The project's Pope at our
  present is **Qian Xun Ji** (95); after his 2631 death, **Bibi Dong**. Bibi
  Dong is canon's **youngest Supreme Pontiff** [canon-wiki] and — see §8 —
  canon's youngest Titled Douluo of her day and youngest 99 Peerless
  [canon-baike, disputed against Tang Hao's "youngest Titled" record].
- **The Worship Hall (Consecration Hall, 供奉殿):** headed by the **Grand
  Worship / Grand Priest**. Our present: **Qian Daoliu, 99** [canon-baike +
  `CANON_GROUND.md`]. The seven great **Worshipers (priests)** serve there —
  all **level 96+**; they **take orders only from the Grand Priest** (and, in
  Renxue's era, the "Young Master" Renxue) [canon-baike/fandom].
- **The vote math (load-bearing for our Ch02–Ch03):** the **Pope holds three
  votes**; **each priest holds one**; **all seven priests together can depose
  the Pope** [fandom Martial Soul Hall]. The Elder Hall governs beside them.
- **The Elder Hall:** roughly **twenty seats**, held by **Titled Douluo
  elders** (the wiki: "20+ Title Douluo elders originally") [fandom]. Hall
  sessions, seats, and robes are drawn in our chapters under this structure.
- **Four Platinum Bishops** — senior prelates over Hall regions: one in
  **Martial Soul City (Spirit City)**, one in **Star Luo City**, **Salas** in
  **Heaven Dou City** (main hall master), **Meyers** over the **Gengxin
  branch** [fandom].
- **Armed orders** [fandom]:
  - **Temple Knights** — level 51+, stationed in both capitals' Main Halls
    under the bishops.
  - **Douluo Warriors** — level 61+, the elders' direct force; commander a
    Titled Douluo.
  - **Holy Emperor Warriors** — level 61+, the Pope's direct force.
- **The hall ladder** [fandom]: **Consecration (Worship) Hall > Papal Hall >
  Sacred Halls (one per capital) > Main Halls (great cities) > Sub-Halls
  (medium cities) > branch halls (smallest cities)** — the structure the
  story's Chenghe arc hangs on.
- **The Hall's public functions:** it runs the **awakening ceremonies** for
  the continent's children and the registration of soul masters [canon-wiki +
  our Ch01–Ch03 usage]; its academy is the continent's best.
- **The academy and the Golden Generation:** Spirit Hall Academy's elite
  trio, the **Golden Generation** — core **Hu Liena**, Bibi Dong's disciple
  and designated successor, raised like a daughter [canon-baike]. (Source
  trap: a Baike passage about the Golden Generation sits inside the Bibi Dong
  entry; it describes **Hu Liena**, not Bibi — tagged and read as such here.)
