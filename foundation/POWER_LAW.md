## The ladder

- Soul masters rank 1–99. Every ten ranks is a wall; a ring is taken at each
  ten. **Title Douluo** is rank 90 and up — the ninth ring. [canon]
- **Titled lifespans run ~300 years.** High ranks touch the Spirit Abyss and
  the Spirit Domain and are blessed by laws; **Limit Douluo** (99) work with
  heaven-and-earth laws themselves. [canon]
- The OC stands at **96** — one level past his canon 95, and worth far more
  than the number: see "why 96 here is not 96 anywhere else". [design/era]

## Rings

- Colors by age: **white 10, yellow 100, purple 1,000, black 10,000, red
  100,000 (1 lakh)** years; **orange / orange-gold 200,000+ (2 lakh+)** — the
  beasts that old are called great soul beasts. [canon + R10]
- Absorption optima this serial keeps: **first ring ≤423 years, second ≤764**
  [canon receipts]; rings show **true colors** — nothing hides a purple as a
  yellow. [canon-strict, k05]
- His rings: **two yellow, two purple, five black** — the canon set, now
  perfectly absorbed in seclusion: part of the body, not jewelry. [canon + R2]

## Bones

- Six positions: head, torso, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg; plus
  rare **external** bones. The torso is strongest; once absorbed a bone
  cannot be separated unless the owner is killed (it drops), except a Titled
  Douluo may sever a limb and force an extraction at the cost of ten levels.
  [canon]
- **Natural effects vs real skills are two different things:** every bone
  gives attribute bonuses (attack, defense, health, growth, vitality,
  recovery) — those are natural effects; the **skill** comes from the beast
  the bone came from and never changes. 10,000-year bones carry one skill;
  100,000-year bones at least two. Skull and arm bones tend to active skills;
  the rest tend to passive. [canon + house ruling 2026-09-27]
- His set so far (canon-strict: fewer than three pieces among the Angel set):
  **head and torso**, each 99,999 years → 100,000 after the seclusion
  (perfect absorption). The other four positions are not yet possessed; they
  will be hunted for later — a 100k hunt guarantees the needed drop. The
  Angel Suit's six bones fuse to 100k each when complete: Angel Sacred Armor
  and Angel Sword, per the Angel God trials. [canon + R2]

## The soul core — the era's only one

- **What canon says:** soul cores are a later-era thing. First core at rank
  90 in the SL2 era, 70+ in the SL3 era; second at 99. Two cores resonate
  (Isotopic Resonance), or oppose as Yin and Yang. Blood Essence Core — the
  Dragon Core family (Tang Wulin, chest, age 59) — forms from bloodline
  concentration. [canon, SL2/SL3 — carried here as the OC's meta knowledge]
- **What this era says [era]:** no human in this era has a soul core. Not
  Qian Daoliu at 99; not Golden Crocodile at 98. The Pope is the first, and
  the elders' shock is canon-true to the era (R7).
- **The True Angel Core [design, R9]:** blood essence + divine things +
  martial soul authority domain — the angel's counterpart of the True Dragon
  Core. It formed in the one-month seclusion at 95→96. In the chest and
  dantian: warm, solid, turning, drinking the world's power. It purifies
  body, meridians, bones; makes the spiritual sea tough as an angel's; it is
  his foundation toward godhood.

## Why 96 here is not 96 anywhere else

No human in this era has a soul core, and he does; his martial soul is divine;
his spiritual power stands very high; his bloodline runs purer than any Qian
before him. His recovery and combat capacity are simply a different order.
That is a **quality** fact, not vanity: the story may use it, but nothing in
it breaks the ladder — he has not skipped a rank and does not get one free.
(R13, k05)
