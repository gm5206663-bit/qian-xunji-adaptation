## B1 · Spirit Hall (武魂殿) — the whole structure

Source: 百度百科 武魂殿 (335050) · 搜狗/微信百科 mirror · 163 article on 长老殿 vs
供奉殿 · 起点 Q&A on membership.

**The four inner halls, and what each one actually is:**

| Hall | 中文 | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pope Hall** | 教皇殿 | the most magnificent building on the continent; the reason Spirit City is every soul master's holy place. Outward-facing: certification of soul masters, disputes between them |
| **Elder Hall** | 长老殿 | **the power core.** Stands between the Pope Hall and the Douluo Hall. Decides all major matters. **Twenty elders, every one a Titled Douluo.** In a vote **the Pope himself holds only three votes**, and if the elders pass a resolution together **they can depose the Pope** |
| **Douluo Hall** | 斗罗殿 | the highest thing in Spirit Hall: **only a dead Titled Douluo may enter.** It is their tomb. At every great rite the Pope comes to its doors to pay respect — and even the Pope, until he dies, has no right to go inside |
| **Worship Hall / Hall of Offerings** | 供奉殿 | **above the Pope Hall.** Only Douluo-rank may enter. Its seven 供奉 are 96+ and **answer to no Pope** |

**The local ladder** (our chapters' "Main Halls" and "branch halls" are canon):

武魂圣殿 Sacred Hall — the two imperial capitals · 武魂主殿 Main Hall — every
large city · 武魂子殿 Sub Hall — every medium city · 武魂分殿 Branch Hall — every
city on the continent, in both empires, each the landmark building of its town ·
plus village-level offices.

**The clergy ranks and their pay** (recorded because a codex-era story needs to
know what a man earns): 白金主教 Platinum Bishop (a Sacred Hall master — Salas
萨拉斯 at the Heaven Dou Sacred Hall) ~45,000 gold soul coins · 黑衣主教
Black-robed Bishop (provincial) ~30,000 · 紫衣主教 Purple-robed (city) ~20,000 ·
红衣主教 Red-robed ~ · 高级白衣执事 senior white-robed deacon (county) ~10,000 ·
低级白衣执事 junior (village) ~5,000. The Pope's own stipend: 200,000.

**Three warrior corps:** 圣殿武士团 guards the two Sacred Halls (the weakest of
the three) · 圣皇武士团 guards the Pope Hall · 斗罗武士团 guards the Douluo Hall.
The last two are equal in standing and **both are led by Titled Douluos.**

**Spirit City (武魂城)** is built around a hill **on the border between the two
empires, and neither empire holds title to it.**

**What Spirit Hall actually does for the continent** (why it is not simply a
villain): it performs every child's martial-soul awakening at age six through its
branch offices; it certifies soul-master rank and issues the certificates; and
**it confers the titles of Titled Douluo.** It is the infrastructure of the whole
soul-master world.

**Origin:** Spirit Hall was **founded by the Angel God.** After the Angel God
ascended to the God Realm, the **千 family — the Six-Winged Angel bloodline —
took it over**, and each generation supplies both the Pope and the 大供奉 whose
one purpose is to find the Angel God's inheritor.

## B3 · The Angel God (天使神) and the inheritance — the lever our story sits on

Source: 百度百科 天使神 (63195449) · 百度百科 千仞雪 (3427715) · novel ch287
(天使第九考) · 腾讯新闻 on the three successions.

- The Angel God is a **first-generation first-class god of the Douluo God
  Realm**, the **Light God (光明神)**, and **the opposite of the Rakshasa God
  (罗刹神)**. He is the source of the Six-Winged Angel bloodline.
- The first Angel God **was human**: he drew the Angel Holy Sword that sealed the
  world's evils and diseases, and the drawing caused catastrophe; he spent
  himself removing evil to atone, and **became a god through the faith he
  earned.** *(Note for our Ch02: the OC's private theory that stored faith can
  raise something is not idle — it is how the first Angel God was made.)*
- Artifacts: **天使圣剑 Angel Holy Sword** and **天使神装 / 天使圣铠** (a soul-bone
  set). After apotheosis the ring set gains a **golden god-rank ring**: 千仞雪's
  became **6 black, 3 red, 1 gold**, the tenth ring golden at the million-year
  level.
- **The succession is by blood, not selection.** Unlike the Sea God's or Asura's
  trials, the Angel God's position **can only pass to a descendant carrying the
  Six-Winged Angel martial soul — that is, the 千 family and no one else.** The
  family guarded the qualification and let no outsider near it.
- **The trials are nine (天使九考). The ninth requires the guardian (护道人) — a
  Limit Douluo — to sacrifice himself to open the door of succession.** 千道流
  burned his own soul power, body and soul to open it for 千仞雪 (ch287). 波塞西
  did the same for Tang San at the Sea God's trials.
- **千仞雪**: daughter of 千寻疾 and 比比东; granddaughter of 千道流; martial soul
  六翼天使; soul-bone set 天使神装; weapon 天使圣剑. She broke with Bibi Dong and
  accepted the nine trials at **ch198**, and became the Angel God at **ch287**.
  Her god position **shattered** at the Jialing Pass when she saved Bibi Dong,
  dropping her to **rank 89 forever, unable ever to reach 90 again.**
- **After her position shattered, the Angel God's seat returned to the God Realm
  and has stayed vacant** — the sword lost, the high priest dead, the faith gone.
  No Angel God has appeared since (confirmed in 神界传说).
- **What this means for our story, and it is the largest thing in this batch:**
  the succession runs through the 千 blood. Our OC *is* a 千 — Qian Xun Ji, son
  of Qian Daoliu, carrying the Six-Winged Angel. Canon's inheritor is his
  daughter. The blood qualifies **both**, the guardian is his own father, and the
  guardian's whole existence exists to find the inheritor. Nothing here is
  decided — R22–R24 still stand and the far era is unlocked — but the door is
  canon, it is unlocked by blood, and it is standing in his own house.

## B6 · Martial souls (武魂) — types, grades, release

Source: 百度百科 斗罗大陆用语列表 (55230119) · 163 article on the classification ·
起点/新浪 summaries.

**Everyone awakens at six.** Innate soul power caps at 10. A soul normally
inherits from **one parent**, which is why the great lines are families.

**The three classes:**

| Class | 中文 | Release | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Beast** | 兽武魂 | **possession (附体)** — the body takes the beast's features | subdivides into 魂兽类 (beasts: 蓝电霸王龙, 柔骨兔, 邪眸白虎 — carnivores strongest), 特殊类 (rare: 残梦, 鬼魅, 幽灵), **神祇类 (divine: 六翼天使, 海神) — the topmost tier of all** |
| **Tool** | 器武魂 | **always external (离体)** | 武器类 weapons (昊天锤, 七杀剑, 龙杖) and 宝物类 treasures (七宝琉璃塔, gems — mostly support). **Plant souls count as tool souls.** Endless variety, some unclassifiable |
| **Body** | 本体武魂 | strengthens the body or one organ | **does not appear in SL1** — first in SL2 with 灵眸. The more vital the organ, the stronger. Unique in having a **second awakening**, graded 黑铁 +30% · 青铜 +60% · 白银 +100% · 黄金 +100–300% |

- **True body (真身):** a tool soul's highest state is **器魂真身** — the soul
  itself merges into the weapon, man and tool one. A beast soul's **兽魂真身**
  does *not* merge the soul; it only materializes the beast.
- **A few** beast-soul holders who kill a beast of their own species for their
  first ring gain **武魂拟态** — an external release form.
- **双生武魂 dual souls:** extremely rare; each takes its own rings; only one may
  be used at a time. **伴生武魂 companion souls:** two similar souls sharing one
  ring's skills — stronger than a single soul, weaker than a dual, and both can
  be used at once.
- **Grades.** The novel's own working words are 废武魂 (trash), 顶级武魂
  (top-grade) and 神级武魂 (god-level — the 神祇类 beast souls). The finer
  nine-step ladder that circulates (神级 > 顶级 > 强大 > 普通强大 > 普通 > 上等 >
  中等 > 下等 > 弱小) is a **reader's tier list, not a canon table** — marked
  [secondary] and never to be quoted as the book's.

**For our pages:** the Six-Winged Angel is a **神祇类 beast soul — the topmost
class of martial soul that exists**, which is why the Angel blood holds the
papacy and the god's succession both. Our Ch01–04 usage is receipted.

## B7 · Soul bones (魂骨) — the six, the external, the odds

Source: 起点 Q&A on 魂骨排名 · 潇湘 Q&A · 游民星空/52pk summaries.

- **Six positions, ranked by preciousness in this order: 躯干 torso > 头 head >
  左臂 left arm > 右臂 right arm > 左腿 left leg > 右腿 right leg.**
- **A dead soul beast leaves a bone about once in a thousand.**
- A bone is **not capped by the beast's age limit** and **evolves as its owner
  grows.** Bones **do not vanish when the owner dies** — which is why they are
  taken from the dead.
- **外附魂骨 (external bone):** not one of the six; its position is not fixed;
  rarer still. On the soul-master world's **dream list it ranks second — below
  only the 100,000-year soul ring, which is treated as near-impossible.** Known:
  Tang San's **八蛛矛** (from the 人面魔蛛, evolved seven times, later the 海神八翼)
  · Hu Liena's **狐尾** · **Bibi Dong's 六翅紫光翼.**
- 大师's three classes: ordinary bones, external bones, **top-grade bones.**
- Bibi Dong's **left and right arm bones both came from the same 100,000-year
  死亡蛛皇.**

**For our pages:** Ch02's "six places a bone could sit — head, torso, both arms,
both legs" is canon, and the ranking above should be used when the codex teaches
it. The bone-fills-what-is-empty rule (Ch02) is canon.

## B9 · 千仞雪 Qian Renxue — the whole arc, and the calendar that holds our story

Source: 百度百科 千仞雪 (3427715) · 百度百科 唐三 (9287143, citing novel ch175/176) ·
快懂百科 · 微信/搜狗百科 mirrors.

**The calendar, settled — and it validates our clock:**

- **Tang San was born 斗罗历 2631年, on the 11th day of the first month**
  (baike, citing the novel). He awakened at six in **斗罗历 2637**, with dual
  souls 蓝银草/蓝银皇 and 昊天锤, innate full soul power, at Nodin Academy under
  玉小刚.
- **千仞雪 was born 斗罗历 2620.** She awakened at six (2626) with the
  **六翼天使** and **innate full soul power of 20** — not 10; the divine-class
  soul doubles the ceiling.
- **So our story's window is canon-correct:** our chapters run 2619 → 2620, she
  is born in 2620, Tang San is born eleven years later in 2631. The "≈11 years
  before Tang San" line in `TIMELINE.md` stands, and now it is receipted.
- **Recorded conflict:** one encyclopedia dates her failed coup to 斗罗历 **2469**,
  which cannot be reconciled with a 2620 birth. The 2631 birth of Tang San
  carries a chapter citation; the 2469 date carries none. **2620/2631 is used;
  2469 is logged as a bad source and not to be cited.**

**Her life, in order:**

1. Born 2620 to 比比东 and 千寻疾; granddaughter of 千道流. Soul: 六翼天使.
   **Innate full soul power 20.**
2. **Age 9 (2629):** sent secretly by 千道流 into the Heaven Dou imperial palace.
   She took the identity of the eldest prince **雪清河**, using a **disguise soul
   skill from a fused piece of the Angel soul-bone set** — not merely makeup or a
   lie. (The donghua adds a real-prince/switched-prince layer; the novel's own
   account is the disguise skill.)
3. In Heaven Dou she **poisoned the second and third princes**. At fourteen she
   killed the twelve-year-old second prince with a slow poison.
4. **Emperor 雪夜 made her crown prince**, and **宁风致 — master of the Seven
   Treasure Glazed Tile School — became her teacher**, which is where her
   support came from.
5. At her teacher's urging she moved to recruit Tang San after the continental
   advanced-academy tournament preliminaries.
6. Her plot to poison Emperor 雪夜 and take the empire **failed — detected by
   雪星王.** She used the failure to **break openly with Bibi Dong**, seized
   control of the **Elder Hall and the Douluo Hall**, and took the post of
   **裁决长老 Judgement Elder.**
7. She opened the **天使九考** and **completed the nine trials in six years**,
   becoming the Angel God at **ch287** — pushed through the final crisis by her
   feeling for Tang San, which is also the crack in her.
8. Titles, gear and end: **武魂殿少主 → 长老殿圣女**; soul-bone set **天使神装**;
   weapon **天使圣剑**; kills 太阳真火 and "大日，天使，万日凌天". Rank 100 as a
   first-class god. At the Jialing Pass she **shattered her own god position to
   save Bibi Dong**, dropping to **rank 89 forever, never again able to reach
   90.** She never entered the God Realm. She was **35** at the end of SL1.
9. **The Angel line's properties:** the Angel God carries **holy and fire**
   attributes and can draw on the sun's power; the god position limits even a
   god to borrowing only a small part of **太阳真火**.
10. **Scale, for perspective:** the whole continent holds only on the order of
    **five thousand 魂斗罗** (ranks 81–90) [secondary — a reader's count, not a
    canon line].

**What this gives our story, and what it forbids:**

- She is our OC's daughter, born on the page in 2620, with **innate soul power
  20** — a number the story can state because the books state it.
- Her canon road is: Heaven Dou at nine, the princes, the crown, the coup, the
  break with her mother, the nine trials, the god position, and the shattering.
  **R23 stands — none of it is pre-decided here, and none of it may be
  pre-decided on a page.** This batch exists so the road is *known*, not so it
  can be written early.
- The disguise skill came from a piece of the Angel soul-bone set. Our OC holds
  two pieces of that set already (head and torso, per Ch01). That is a fact to
  keep straight, not a lever to pull.
