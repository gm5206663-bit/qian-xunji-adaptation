## 1. What he actually has

### Rank and soul
- **Rank 96.** [story — canon's Xunji is 95 per baike; 96 is the story's fixed
  choice, held across every chapter. NUMBER_LEDGER §1.]
- **Martial soul: 六翼天使 Six-Winged Angel.** [canon] A **神祇类 beast soul —
  the topmost tier of martial soul that exists** (HARVEST B6). It carries the
  Angel God's shape in it, which is why the blood holds both the papacy and the
  god's succession.
- **Six wings.** [canon] Two until seventy, four until ninety, **six after
  ninety** — the old rule of the blood (CANON_MASTER §7).
- **The blood runs purer in him than it has in fifty years** — white-gold, not
  the dim gold of the old man. [story, on the canon premise that the line had
  degraded under a Pope's road.]

### Rings
- **Nine: two yellow, two purple, five black.** [story]
- Measured against canon's own optimum — **two yellow, two purple, four black,
  one red** (CANON_MASTER §3) — his set is the right *shape* for the era, but
  **he has no red ring.** No 100,000-year ring at all. [canon comparison]
- **This is his largest single deficit and it should never be glossed:** his
  father 千道流 holds **six black and three red**, and 唐晨 holds **eight black
  and one red.** On ring quality alone, both are far ahead of him.

### Bones
- **Two pieces of the Angel set — head and torso — at 100,000 years each.**
  [story on the absorption; canon on the positions and the tier]
- The torso is the **first** of the six ranked positions and the head the
  **second** (HARVEST B7) — so he holds the two best seats there are.
- A 100,000-year bone carries **at least two skills** (HARVEST B11).
- He has **no external bone (外附魂骨)** — the thing that ranks second on the
  world's dream list. Bibi Dong has one. He does not. [canon comparison]

### Cores — the anomaly
- **First core, in the dantian.** [story, on canon's mechanism] **Nobody alive
  in this era has one.** The method is not invented until the SL2 era, twenty
  thousand years on, and cores arrive at the Titled wall at the earliest
  (HARVEST B15, CANON_MASTER §2b).
- **Second core, in the spiritual sea between the eyebrows — thin, still
  solidifying.** [story] The dual core is canonically **the marker of the high
  band** in later ages, and 帝天 — the strongest beast alive — carries a yin-yang
  complementary dual core (HARVEST B22).
- **What cores actually buy:** recovery speed and endurance, not a bigger number
  (CANON_MASTER §2b). Two of them mean he can fight longer and harder than his
  rank says he should.

### Spiritual power
- **Spirit Domain, 灵域境, 20,000–49,999 points.** [canon system, story
  placement — CANON_MASTER §14.4]
- Canon's rule: **Spirit Domain is the foundation for Hyper Douluo (95–98)
  cultivation and the road toward Limit Douluo** — and **not every Super Douluo
  reaches it.** An ordinary Titled Douluo sits down near Spirit Abyss.
- **Era rule:** in 2619 no word and no instrument exist for this. He knows his
  standing only because he read it. [canon]

### Domain
- **天使领域, the Angel Domain.** [canon] Holy energy that **purifies and melts
  the enemy's soul power**, slows what stands against him, settles what stands at
  its centre, and washes out death-aura. Skills 天使净化 and 天使降临.
- **Only a Six-Winged Angel soul master can comprehend it**, and **千道流 holds
  it at 99** — so holding it at 96 is inside the pattern, not an invention.
- Domains are rare even among Titled Douluo: of the Sea God Island's seven
  pillars, only three have one (HARVEST B8, B18).

### The sword
- **A plain sword, no name, no history, two hundred mornings of it.** [story]
- The cut lands in the same place twice out of three; he can stop the blade a
  finger's width off a post in the dark, from the held-cut drill.
- **Honest grade: early.** The sword road climbs Intent → Soul → God, and he is
  nowhere near Intent. It is a discipline that keeps his edges hard, not a weapon
  that beats anyone.
- **He does not have the 天使圣剑.** That is canon's artifact of the Angel
  inheritance, and it is not his. [canon]

**Level with him:** the 五, 六 and 七供奉 at 96 — and he should beat all three, on
the cores, the domain, the purified blood and two 100,000-year bones.

**The genuine questions:**
- **大明 and 二明** — 100,000-year beasts, a little under a 99-level Bibi Dong
  and far above an ordinary 95 (B18). Against an ordinary 95 they win. Against a
  96 with two cores and a domain, it is not settled, and no page should settle it
  early.
- **唐昊.** He broke the *old* Pope at 95 while newly Titled at forty-four, using
  大须弥锤 — which condenses nine rings into one blow so that **no ring skill on
  either side matters any more** (B16). That technique is the specific answer to
  everything our OC has built. **The rebuilt man is not the man he broke, but the
  hammer is still the hammer.**
- **Bibi Dong** — in canon she ends at 99 with dual souls, the Slaughter God
  Domain, the Death Domain and an external bone. In 2619 she is the Saintess
  under lock, carrying his child. **R23 forbids pre-deciding her road.**

**So, stated plainly:** in 2619 he is **not the strongest thing on the
continent.** Three Limit Douluo stand above him, and a nine-hundred-thousand-year
dragon in the forest would end him. He is **the strongest man alive under those
three** — which is exactly the claim Chapter 4 puts in his own mouth and
immediately qualifies, as R27 requires.

## 3. What makes him dangerous, and it is not the number

A 96 is a 96. What is without precedent in this era:

1. **Two cores.** Nobody alive has one. Recovery and endurance that his rank does
   not account for.
2. **Spiritual power at Spirit Domain,** which most Super Douluo never reach —
   and spiritual power is what governs resistance to mental attack, perception,
   and how much a body can carry.
3. **The Angel Domain,** which drains the enemy's soul power while the fight goes
   on. Combined with two cores, **time is on his side in any long fight** — he
   recovers faster than they drain, and they drain faster than they recover.
4. **He knows everything.** Every ring age, every bone position, every wall,
   every opponent's eventual road, six books deep. In a fight, that is worth more
   than a rank.
