## 2. His two cores — and why the ordinary rules do not apply to them

**This is where all three earlier versions of this file went wrong.**

Baidu Baike's rule — *first core at Titled Douluo, second at 99, and a core does
not increase cultivation* — is the rule for **ordinary soul-power cores.**

**Neither of his cores is one.**

### The True Angel Core
**Blood essence + divine things + martial soul authority domain.** The angel's
counterpart of the **True Dragon Core.** [design, R9]

Formed in the one-month seclusion at 95→96 — **not condensed by the SL2 method.**
It sits in the chest and dantian: warm, solid, turning, drinking the world's
power. It **purifies body, meridians and bones**, makes the spiritual sea tough
as an angel's, and is his foundation toward godhood.

**The receipt that matters, and the one I kept missing:** **Blood Essence cores
—the Dragon Core family — run PARALLEL to soul-power cores, not under their
limits.** (`POWER_LAW.md`, receipts in `CANON_MASTER.md` §2b.) So the "second
core belongs at 99" rule does not bind him, and judging him by it was simply the
wrong rulebook.

**Phenomena on formation:** golden vortex, white-gold ripples, six wings faint
inside it, an angel's pupil, a holy pillar of light from the sun, the Angel
Domain expanding.

### The second core — the Spirit Core
Built from a **sword consciousness: spiritual power fused with the sword.**
[R27, the author's order] It sits in the **spiritual sea between the brows.**
Cold, turning against the first. Rank stays 96.

**The sword ladder it rests on is canon:** Intent → Soul → God (SL3
ch1249/1515), with SL2's Ji Juechen as the fused-spirit receipt (ch271). No
tool-soul speaks.

**So: he does not "condense soul cores." He has a Blood Essence core of divine
make and a Spirit core of sword-and-spirit make, in an era where no human has
either — or anything like them.**

## 3. The rest of him

- **Rank 96** — one past his canon 95. He did not skip a rank and gets none

  make the climb's *floor* his, not the summit.

- **Bones: head and torso of the Angel set, 100,000 years each.** The torso is
  the strongest of the six positions. Canon-strict: he holds **fewer than three**
  pieces of the Angel set. The other four will be hunted for — a 100,000-year
  hunt guarantees the drop. **When all six are complete they fuse to 100,000
  each: the Angel Sacred Armor and the Angel Sword, per the Angel God trials.**
- **Angel Domain** — innate to those carrying the Seraphim spirit; becomes a
  **God-level domain after god level.**
- **Spiritual power: very high** — 95–96 Titled Douluo, plus the True Angel Core,
  plus the talent's purification. **`POWER_LAW.md` forbids stating a number
  until a chapter fixes one, and no chapter has. So no number is stated here,
  and CANON_MASTER §14.4 has been corrected to match.**
- **Titled lifespans run ~300 years**; a Limit Douluo at 99 works with
  heaven-and-earth laws themselves.
- **Faith power** — the hidden strategy. The codex and the guild exist to gather
  belief quietly, toward a God position of his own.
